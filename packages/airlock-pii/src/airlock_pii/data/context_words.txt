# One word per line, in lower case. How a word is matched:
# - A word of four letters or more is a stem and may carry up to six more letters: Russian
#   and Kazakh put their endings after it, so "улиц" covers "улица" and "улице", and "көшесі"
#   covers "көшесінде".
# - A shorter word, and any word with a full stop or a hyphen, is matched as it is written.
#   So is every word of [verb], [day-or-month], [and] and [before-a-subject].
# - A word of [person], [label] or [name-label] may have a colon after it, with one or two
#   words in between: "Ответственный: Петров", "Секретарь собрания: Ахметова".
# - Case is ignored, except in [organization] and [sole-proprietor]: those are written in
#   capitals, and "АО" is not "ао".
