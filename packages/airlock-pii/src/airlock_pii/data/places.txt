# Cities, regions and countries that business text in Kazakhstan names. Unverified: written
# from general knowledge. See README.md in this folder.
#
# A word of this list is a place and never a person, in its base form, in a Russian case
# form or with a Kazakh case suffix ("Астаны", "Алматыға"). A place that is also a common
# given name or surname is not listed: "Абай", "Сәтбаев" and "Қонаев" are a town and people.
# For those the word next to them decides ("Абай қаласы", "город Сатпаев").
#
# [like-a-surname] is of another kind: towns whose names end like a surname, many of them
# named after a person. Such a word is still a surname next to a given name, initials or a
# word for a person ("Анна Ростова", "клиент Чехов"). Standing alone in the middle of a
# sentence it is the town, and the rule that takes a surname alone for a person leaves it
# ("летим в Ростов", "из Пушкина", "в Кемерове"). A town in -ово, -ево or -ино is listed for
# its other cases, which look like those of a surname.
